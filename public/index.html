How VidyutRakshak Saves Energy

🎙️

Voice Control

Say "Turn off all lights" or "सभी लाइटें बंद करो" — no need to walk to switches. Reduces habit of leaving lights on.

📡

MQTT Automation

ESP32 sensors detect occupancy and gas levels. Lights auto-off when no motion. Gas detected → alert sent instantly.

🔐

RFID Security

No keys needed. RFID cards grant access — no standing outside wasting time. Every entry is logged for safety.

📊

Real-Time Monitoring

Live dashboard shows exactly which devices are on. Helps identify wasted electricity instantly from any device.
